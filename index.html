Why it matters

Anyone can stack bricks. Feature work shows every mistake.

A curved wall, an arch or a patterned bond is unforgiving. Lines drift, joints vary and the whole facade looks off for decades.

Murtagh Masonry is built on the jobs other crews avoid: raked curves, burglar bond, natural arches and restoration on old brickwork.
